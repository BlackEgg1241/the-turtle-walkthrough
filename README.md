# The Turtle — Wooramel Roadhouse bar concept

Interactive, to-scale walkthrough of the proposed bar in the carport beside Wooramel Roadhouse.
The current app opens only the revised 6.7 m daytime roadhouse design, with three standing table sets, white table plants, a continuous right window and an illuminated turtle symbol. The beer slogan board is removed, the marlin is deep blue, and the tall corner plant has a matte black pot.

[Open the current design](https://blackegg1241.github.io/the-turtle-walkthrough/) or [browse all 12 renders](https://blackegg1241.github.io/the-turtle-walkthrough/roadhouse-renders.html), including the corner detail, link entrance and overall viewpoints. Photo mode includes three matching panoramas. Earlier concept files are archived and are not offered in the current app.

Open `index.html` (or the GitHub Pages URL): walk with W A S D and the mouse, or use the viewpoints panel.
Photo mode shows Cycles-rendered 360° views. Built in Blender from site photos; furniture and textures are CC0 (Poly Haven), people are cutouts from skalgubbar.se, landscape prints are Creative Commons from Wikimedia Commons.

Vehicle model: "Toyota Land Cruiser" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/toyota-land-cruiser-91b5815c64eb43b0a88f6fdb9df774e4) by Renafox (https://sketchfab.com/kryik1023), licensed CC BY-NC 4.0. Private concept, not for commercial use.
